ASHRAE 62.1 sizes outdoor air in two parts: a people rate (Rp) for the contaminants occupants produce, and an area rate (Ra) for what the building itself gives off. Vbz = Rp x Pz + Ra x Az, and the zone outdoor airflow is Voz = Vbz / Ez. Exhaust minimums from Table 6.2 are a separate check: public toilets 50 CFM per water closet or urinal (continuous), commercial kitchens 0.70 CFM/ft², parking garages 0.75 CFM/ft².
